## 01 — Journey Tree

**Problems**

- High information density makes content difficult to scan.
- Linear navigation limits access to specific steps.

**Solutions**

- Card-based overview with clear step summaries.
- Flexible navigation with direct access to individual steps.

## 02 — Explore / Short Trip

**Problems**

- Limited route customisation.
- Unclear purpose of local exploration.
- Cluttered information and unclear primary actions.
- Confusing gamification feedback.

**Solutions**

- Customisable routes and checkpoints.
- Clearer emphasis on local exploration.
- Simplified information layout and prominent primary actions.
- Removal of XP, Level, and badges.

## 03 — AR Lens

**Problems**

- Too many steps to access practical information.
- Scan results do not immediately provide actionable instructions.

**Solutions**

- Simplified scanning workflow.
- Immediate, action-oriented guidance.

## 04 — Ask This Place

**Problem**

- Location-based recommendations may not match users' actual needs.

**Solution**

- User-controlled location selection for more relevant recommendations.

## 05 — Homepage

**Problems**

- Scenario Simulation is difficult to access directly.
- Key features are separated across different sections.

**Solutions**

- Direct access to Scenario Simulation.
- Integrated shortcuts to essential tools.
